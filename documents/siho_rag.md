# MyTruAdvantage (MTA) Health Plan Chatbot

## Overview

MyTruAdvantage (MTA) Health Plan Chatbot is a friendly, AI-powered chatbot designed to help health plan members and insurance agents quickly find answers to their questions.

Users can chat in English or Spanish to get instant information about plan benefits, check what services are covered, and find contact details for local doctors and pharmacies near them.

## Features

### 1. Instant Benefit Answers

Answers questions about health insurance coverage, including:

- Dental benefits
- Vision benefits
- Hearing benefits
- Fitness benefits

This helps users understand their health plan without having to read through lengthy policy documents.

### 2. Doctor & Pharmacy Locator

Helps users find doctors and pharmacies close to their home using:

- City
- County
- ZIP code

The chatbot provides useful information such as:

- Addresses
- Phone numbers
- Special services

### 3. Bilingual Support (English & Spanish)

The chatbot supports conversations in both English and Spanish.

It can automatically detect the user's language and remember the conversation history to provide a smooth conversational experience.

### 4. Quick Connection to Human Support

When users need additional assistance, the chatbot can:

- Connect them with customer service.
- Register their enrollment interest.

This provides a direct path from automated assistance to human support when required.

## Key Highlights

- Provides instant support 24/7 for health plan and coverage questions.
- Helps members find local doctors and pharmacies in seconds.
- Chats naturally in both English and Spanish.
- Directly connects members to customer support staff when extra help is needed.
