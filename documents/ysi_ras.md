# YSIRas (Youngsoft India Recruitment Assessment System)

## Overview

YSIRas is a modern, AI-powered hiring and video interview platform designed to simplify talent acquisition.

It automates time-consuming recruiting activities, including analyzing job descriptions, screening applicant resumes, scheduling secure video interviews, and evaluating candidate skills.

The platform provides hiring managers and recruitment teams with a collaborative dashboard to coordinate vacancies, monitor screening progress, and export comprehensive assessment reports, helping organizations hire top talent up to 70% faster.

## Features

### 1. Smart Job Requirement Analyzer

Automatically reviews uploaded job descriptions and identifies important requirements, including:

* Required technical skills
* Minimum years of experience
* Educational qualifications

These requirements establish a clear benchmark for evaluating and screening candidates.

### 2. AI Resume Screening & Ranking

Scans applicant resumes and compares them against the requirements of an open position.

Candidates are scored and ranked based on how closely their:

* Experience
* Education
* Skills

align with the job requirements.

### 3. Integrated Video Interview Rooms

Creates secure, high-definition video meeting rooms directly within the web browser.

Candidates and recruiters can conduct face-to-face interviews without installing external applications or downloading additional software.

### 4. Secure, Self-Expiring Interview Links

Generates private and secure interview invitation links for shortlisted candidates.

The links automatically expire after a configured period, such as one week, helping maintain interview schedules and improve security.

### 5. Automated Candidate Evaluation

Provides immediate, structured evaluations of candidate competencies after an interview.

Evaluations can cover areas such as:

* Technical skills
* Communication
* Problem-solving

The system provides a standardized scorecard to help hiring teams consistently assess candidates.

### 6. Hiring Team Collaboration Hub

Provides a centralized dashboard for recruitment teams and hiring managers.

Teams can:

* Assign jobs to recruiters
* Monitor applicant status
* Track recruiting metrics
* Monitor resumes reviewed
* Track interviews scheduled
* Track offers sent
* Download candidate reports

### 7. Applicant Tracking System (ATS) Integration

Integrates with existing HR software and Applicant Tracking Systems.

Candidate information, status updates, and evaluation scores can be synchronized between the platforms.

## Key Highlights

* Reduces manual resume screening and filtering time by more than 80%.
* Standardizes interviewer evaluations and scores to support consistent candidate assessment.
* Provides secure candidate screening through single-use meeting links and expiration controls.
* Includes a collaborative dashboard for recruiting teams and hiring managers.
* Provides a clean experience for both candidates and recruiters across devices.
