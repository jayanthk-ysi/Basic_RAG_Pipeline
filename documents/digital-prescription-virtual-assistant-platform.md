# Digital Prescription & Virtual Assistant Platform

## Overview

The Digital Prescription & Virtual Assistant Platform modernizes medication management by connecting clinicians with pharmacy networks and real-time electronic prescribing tools.

**Client:** e-Prescribing Network  
**Demo:** https://h2hsolutions.com/drx-virtual-assitance/  
**Type:** Predictive Analytics

## Features

- Digital Prescription Management
- Controlled Substances Prescribing
- Prescription Monitoring Integration
- Automated Prior Authorization
- Clinical Decision Support
- Medication History & Eligibility Verification
- Real-Time Prescription Benefit & Formulary Intelligence
- AI-Powered Virtual Assistance
- AI Medication Adherence Prediction
- Practice Software Integration

## Key Capabilities

### Digital Prescription Management
Enables providers to create, manage, track, renew, cancel, and transmit electronic prescriptions.

### Clinical Decision Support
Performs drug-drug, drug-allergy, drug-food, duplicate therapy, and dosage interaction checks.

### Prior Authorization
Supports submission, review, and approval workflows directly within the prescribing workflow.

### Medication Intelligence
Provides medication history, insurance eligibility, formulary information, and prescription benefit information.

### AI Virtual Assistance
Provides conversational support for workflow navigation and operational questions.

### Medication Adherence Prediction
Uses predictive analytics to identify patients at risk of medication non-adherence.

## Workflow

```text
Provider
 ↓
Patient / Medication Information
 ↓
Prescription Workflow
 ↓
Eligibility / Formulary / Drug Checks
 ↓
Prior Authorization if Required
 ↓
Electronic Prescription
 ↓
Pharmacy Network
```

## Business Value

- Reduces staff time spent on insurance and pharmacy phone calls.
- Supports faster prior authorization workflows.
